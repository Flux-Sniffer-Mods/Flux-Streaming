# Flux Streaming changes

## An app of its own

Streaming was a module of Flux Titan Hub. It's now Flux Streaming, an app of its own on Flux Core
(what the Flux apps share), with its own updates and About page.

- **Your pairings come along**: the phone's identity to Moonlight, the devices paired with it, the
  TV and your choices are taken over from Flux Keyboard (once it's updated), so nothing pairs again.
- **Games from Flux Gaming**: Moonlight lists them with their art, opened through Flux Gaming's
  launcher; gaming mode plays them with the keys while they stream, on the phone or the TV's screen.
- **The desktop from Flux Desktop**: in the Streaming app, opened on the TV's screen.
- **The TV's volume on the phone's keys** while it plays there, as a cast app has it (no
  accessibility service needed); in a game, SYM with them, through gaming mode.
- **Moonlight's Wake** listened for from boot; "Always available" keeps it listening.
- **One shell for all the Flux apps**: the built-in shell paired in any of them is used here too.

Everything streaming did before comes along: Moonlight hosting with pairing PINs, mirrored or
separate screens, Android's desktop, apps and Termux scripts in Moonlight's list, the TV turned on
into a game, AirPlay and Miracast.
